# Word lists

`words.txt` and `bigrams.txt` are the SymSpell English frequency dictionaries
(`frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt` and `frequency_bigramdictionary_en_243_342.txt`)
from https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell.
